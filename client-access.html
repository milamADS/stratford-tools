📋 Onboarding: get access the right way

Read this off on the call. You never ask for a password — the client grants your Business Manager access to their ad account. Takes ~5 minutes together.
  1. You: have your own Meta Business Manager ready (business.facebook.com). Set this up once, use it for every client. "I'll never need your password — you just add my business as a partner. You stay in full control."
  2. Client: makes sure they have a Facebook business Page and an ad account. If not, create them together now (free, ~10 min).
  3. Client: goes to their Business Settings → Partners (or "Ad Accounts → Assign Partner"), clicks Add / Assign Partner.
  4. You: give them your Business Manager ID (a number in your Business Settings → Business Info). They paste it in.
  5. Client: selects the ad account (and Page, if you'll manage posts too) and grants your role — Manage campaigns / Advertiser access.
  6. Client: confirms their credit card is on the ad account's Payment Settings. This funds the ads — paid straight to Facebook, never through you.
  7. You: accept the partner request in your Business Manager. Their ad account now appears in your dashboard — ready to build campaigns.
  8. Record it above: add the client, their Ad Account ID, and set status to Access Granted.
🔑 Keys, not the wallet: you log in with YOUR account and manage theirs through permission — you never see their password or card number, and they can revoke you in one click. That's the whole trust promise, made real.

📲 Send-to-client: “How to add me as a partner”

Copy/paste this to the client, or read it while screen-sharing. These are the steps they click on their end. (Meta moves menus around occasionally — wording may differ slightly, but the path is the same.)
  1. Go to business.facebook.com and log in. If you don't have a Business Manager yet, click Create Account and follow the prompts (name = your business, ~2 min).
  2. Click the gear icon ⚙️ (Business Settings) in the bottom-left.
  3. In the left menu, open Users → Partners.
  4. Click the blue Add button, then choose “Give a partner access to your assets.”
  5. Enter my Business Manager ID: 1055861570245020 and click Next.
  6. On the left, pick Ad Accounts, select your ad account, and toggle on “Manage campaigns” (and Pages → your Page → Manage if I'll handle posts too).
  7. Click Save Changes. That's it — I'll get the request on my side and accept it.
💡 Before step 6 works, you need an ad account. If you don't have one: Business Settings → Accounts → Ad Accounts → Add → Create a new ad account, then add your own card under its Payment Settings. That card pays Facebook directly — it never goes through me.
