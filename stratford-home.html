Questions

Fair questions, straight answers

Do you spend my money for me?

No. Your ad budget goes directly from your own account and card to Facebook — it never touches ours. We only charge a separate flat management fee for the work we do. You have full visibility and control over your spend at all times.

What's the free trial?

You get 14 days of management completely free with no lock-in. It's our way of proving the value before you commit to anything — enough time to get your campaign past Facebook's learning phase and start seeing real activity. If it's not a fit, you walk away — no contract, no fee.

How much should I budget for ads?

That depends on your goals and industry — we'll recommend a starting budget on your discovery call. Many small businesses start in the $1,000–$3,000/month range for ad spend, and we help you scale up as results prove out.

How soon will I see results?

Facebook's algorithm needs a short learning period (usually the first week or two) to optimize. Most clients start seeing meaningful leads or sales within the first few weeks, with performance improving as we optimize.

Do I need my own website?

Ideally yes — it gives your ads somewhere to send people and lets us track conversions. If you don't have one, we can point ads to a landing page or your social profile, and help you plan a proper destination.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No long-term contracts, ever. Cancel whenever you like — though we're confident you won't want to once the results start coming in.
