Questions

Fair questions, straight answers

Do you spend my money for me?

No. Your ad budget goes directly from your own account and card to Facebook — it never touches ours. We charge only a separate flat management fee for the work we perform. You retain full visibility and control over your spend at all times.

What is the free trial?

You receive 14 days of management entirely free, with no lock-in. It is our way of proving value before you commit to anything — enough time to move your campaign past Facebook's learning phase and see real activity. If it is not a fit, you walk away — no contract, no fee.

How much should I budget for ads?

That depends on your goals and industry — we recommend a starting budget on your discovery call. Many businesses begin in the $1,000–$3,000 per month range for ad spend, and we help you scale as results prove out.

How soon will I see results?

Facebook's algorithm requires a short learning period — usually the first week or two — to optimize. Most clients begin seeing meaningful leads or sales within the first few weeks, with performance improving as we refine.

What if I don't have Facebook set up yet?

Not a problem at all. Setting up your Facebook business Page and ad account is part of onboarding — we do it together on our first call, it is quick and free, and you own all of it. The only piece that has to be you is adding your own card to the ad account, since that budget is paid directly to Facebook.

Do you need my password or my card details?

No — and that is by design. We never ask for your Facebook password or your card number. You simply grant our business account permission to manage your ad account, and you can revoke that access in one click at any time. Your login and payment details stay entirely yours.

Why not just run the ads myself?

You could — Facebook does not charge for access. Our fee is for the expertise that turns that ad budget into actual customers instead of wasted spend. Most self-run campaigns lose money to poor targeting, weak creative, and no tracking. Think of it like an accountant: you could file your own taxes, but the professional pays for themselves.

Do I need my own website?

Ideally, yes — it gives your ads a destination and allows us to track conversions. If you do not have one, we can direct ads to a landing page or your social profile, and help you plan a proper destination.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No long-term contracts, ever. Cancel whenever you wish — though we are confident you will not want to once results begin to arrive.
