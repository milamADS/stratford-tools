🚀 Client Onboarding

Work top to bottom for every new client. Tap items to check them off (saves in your browser).

Phase 1 — Paperwork & Payment ▶
  • Signed agreement / scope (what's included, fee, no-lock trial terms)
  • Sent "How We Handle Your Money" one-pager
  • Stripe payment link sent + first payment / trial confirmed
  • Confirmed billing contact + email for receipts
Phase 2 — Access ▶

The model: the CLIENT owns everything, we get access. They pay Facebook directly.

  • Client has a Meta Business Manager
  • Client has an Ad Account inside THEIR Business Manager
  • Client's card is the payment method on their ad account
  • We're granted Partner access to their ad account
  • Access to their Facebook Page
  • Access to Instagram (if running IG ads)
  • Correct access level confirmed (advertise/manage)
Phase 3 — Tracking ▶
  • Meta Pixel created in their ad account
  • Pixel installed on the website (Shopify / WordPress / paste in head)
  • Key conversion events defined (Purchase / Lead / Contact / Booking)
  • Test event fired & confirmed in Events Manager
  • GA4 connected (optional — total site traffic)
  • Service biz: agreed how they'll report closed jobs (close rate × job value)
Phase 4 — Creative Assets ▶
  • Client photo/video folder received (real work beats stock)
  • Logo files (transparent PNG ideal)
  • Brand colors + fonts
  • The offer nailed down (what's the ad promising?)
  • Testimonials / reviews to feature
  • Canva set up for building creative
Phase 5 — Strategy & Launch ▶
  • Goal defined (leads / purchases / calls / bookings)
  • Target audience defined (location, age, interests / lookalike)
  • Ad budget set (their spend, separate from our fee)
  • First campaign built (2–4 variations to test)
  • Client approved creative + copy
  • Launched ✅
  • Reporting cadence agreed
Phase 6 — Ongoing Management ▶
  • Weekly: what's spending, what's converting, what's dead
  • Pause losers, scale winners
  • Refresh creative every few weeks (ad fatigue)
  • Send report on schedule (Spend → Visits → Leads/Sales → Revenue → ROAS)
  • Log any client complaints → Pain Log

💳 Stripe / Payments

Automatic monthly billing, zero friction for the client.

Part 1 — Create & verify (one time)

Go to stripe.com → Sign up (free; they take a % per charge).
Business details: name Stratford Advertisements, type (sole proprietor is fine), address, DOB.
Add your bank account for payouts + SSN/EIN.
Stripe verifies you (instant to a day). Then you can accept live payments.
💡 Build everything in Test mode first (toggle top-right), then flip to Live.

Part 2 — Build the monthly product

Dashboard → Product catalog → + Add product

  • Name: "Meta Ads Management — Growth Plan"
  • Price: your monthly fee (e.g. $1,500)
  • Billing period: Monthly / Recurring
No-lock first month: on the price, set a 30-day free trial (client enters card now, first charge after 30 days, cancel anytime) — or a 100%-off-first-month coupon. Free-trial route is cleanest.

Part 3 — Get paid (easiest)

Dashboard → Payment Links → + New
Select your subscription product + turn on the trial.
Create link → get a buy.stripe.com/xxxxx URL.
Send it to the client. They enter card once → auto-charges monthly. One link reusable for all clients on that plan.

Fees (build into pricing)

  • ~2.9% + $0.30 per card charge (on $1,500 ≈ $44).
  • ACH bank debit ~0.8% — cheaper for big fees, enable as an option; clears in a couple days.

The "how to pay" client blurb

Getting started is simple. Click the secure link below to set up your monthly management fee — takes about a minute. You'll enter payment details once, and your first month is on us (no lock-in — cancel anytime). 👉 [Set up your plan]. A quick note: your ad budget always stays on your own card and is paid directly to Facebook — we never touch it. This link only covers our flat management fee.

🎯 Pixel Verification

Selecting a Pixel in Ads Manager isn't enough — it has to be installed and firing on the site, or you're flying blind. Verify BEFORE spending.

Current client Pixel: "milam capital group pixel" — ID 1571984127698592

Step 1 — Is it firing? (Events Manager)

Ads Manager → menu → Events Manager.
Click the Pixel → Overview. Green/recent activity = live ✅. "No activity" = not installed ⚠️.

Step 2 — Live test (the sure way)

Events Manager → Pixel → Test Events tab.
Enter the site URL → Open Website → browse it.
Events should pop up in real time. Nothing = code isn't on the site ❌.
⚡ Fastest check: the Meta Pixel Helper Chrome extension. Visit the site, click the icon, green = firing.

Step 3 — If not installed

  • Shopify → add the Facebook & Instagram channel (one-click).
  • WordPress → PixelYourSite or the official Meta plugin.
  • Squarespace / Wix → built-in Pixel field, paste the ID.
  • Custom site → paste base code in <head> or use Google Tag Manager.
  • Client's web person → email them the base code from Events Manager.

Step 4 — Money events

Set the action that = a customer: Purchase (e-commerce) or Lead / Contact / Booking (service). Then re-test.

30-sec sanity check before ANY campaign: (1) recent activity ✅ (2) PageView fires on visit ✅ (3) your money-event fires ✅. All green = safe to spend.

Canva workflow

  • Feed: 1080×1080 or 1080×1350 · Stories/Reels: 1080×1920
  • Client photo → logo → offer text. Keep on-image text minimal (Meta suppresses text-heavy images).
  • Export PNG/JPG, make 2–4 variations to test.

Copy formulas

PAS: [Problem]. [Why it's frustrating]. [Solution + offer].
"Roof leaking again? Patch jobs never last. Get a free inspection + honest quote this week."
Straightforward: [Who you help] — [what you do] — [offer/CTA].
"Homeowners in [City]: get a free roof inspection. Book in 30 seconds."
Social proof: "[Result]." Join [#] happy customers. [Offer].

CTAs: Get a free quote · Book now · Claim your offer · Message us

Before you publish

  • Scroll-stopping visual (bright, real person/product)
  • One clear message (not 3 offers)
  • Hook/offer in the first line
  • Obvious CTA
  • Minimal on-image text
  • Looks good on mobile (90%+ of views)
  • 2–4 variations to test
Testing rhythm: launch 2–4 creatives → give it a few days + budget → pause losers, scale winners → refresh every 2–4 weeks to beat ad fatigue.
