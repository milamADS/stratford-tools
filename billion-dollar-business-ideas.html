🚀 Part 1 — Leverage Your Ads Skills

Highest-fit, lowest-risk. You already own the core competency. Don't skip these chasing oil glamour — this is where your unfair advantage lives.

🛢️ Part 2 — Into Oil & Energy

Honest framing: owning wells/refineries needs millions — not a realistic start. But real low-capital doors exist, and most reward relationships, hustle & sales skill. Your edge: grandfather Christopher Milam's network = access, mentorship, a foot in the door.

📈 Part 3 — High-Ceiling Wealth Builders

The real wealth-builders. Higher capital or partners needed, but the biggest long-term upside.

🎯 The Honest Bottom Line

✅ Do first — skills you have, low risk

  • Productize + niche the agency (1.1, 1.2)
  • Build a lead-gen asset you own (1.3)
  • If oil calls you: market to the energy industry now (2.2) & explore landman work (2.1) — both cheap, both use your strengths, both leverage your grandfather's network.

🔨 Build toward — 2–5 yrs, agency cash cushioning risk

  • Info product once you have a real track record (1.5)
  • DTC brand or a first business acquisition (1.4, 3.1)
  • Start stacking assets: real estate, small mineral interests (3.2, 2.3)

🌙 Aspirational — needs partners or real capital

  • B2B / vertical SaaS, oil & gas software (1.6, 2.5, 3.3)
  • Business acquisition at scale (3.1)

✕ Cross off for now — fantasy without a fortune or credentials

  • Energy trading (2.6)
  • Owning wells / large mineral packages / oilfield service fleets from scratch
The unifying principle: Your ad agency is a cash-flow and skill engine. The winning play isn't to abandon it for a shinier industry — it's to (1) make it scalable, (2) use it to serve/enter industries you want (like energy), and (3) redeploy the profits into owned assets: leads, software, businesses, minerals, real estate. Get into oil through relationships and skills first (landman, energy marketing), not by buying assets you can't yet afford.
⚠️ Reality note: All capital figures & salary bands are ballpark and cyclical (especially oil, which swings with commodity prices). Verify current numbers before acting. Sources: landmancareer.com, SBA.gov 7(a) program, industry framing on productized vs. custom agency models.
